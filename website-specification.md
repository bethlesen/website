## Executive Summary

Conversion-focused website for higher education executive coaching and consulting business serving three audience segments: early career professionals, middle managers, and senior leaders.

**Primary Goal:** 85% email list conversion rate
**Platform:** Zoho Sites with full Zoho ecosystem integration
**Brand Position:** 6.5/10 formality - premium executive service meets trusted resource
**Launch Date:** January 26, 2026

---

## Business Overview

### Mission
Executive coaching and consulting for higher education professionals navigating complex workplace challenges sustainably.

### Core Value Proposition
Counter the misconception that higher education careers require sacrifice of wellbeing. Position higher ed as financially viable and fulfilling career path.

### Services
1. Early Career Coaching
2. Middle Manager Coaching
3. Leadership Coaching
4. Team Development
5. Culture Consulting
6. Strategic Planning
7. Speaking Engagements
8. Campus Workshops
9. Group Coaching Cohorts
10. Retreats

### Revenue Streams
- Individual coaching
- Group cohorts (limited enrollment with payment)
- Book sales (direct and retail)
- Digital products (workbooks, webinars)
- Speaking and workshop fees

## Target Audience & User Segments

### Segment 1: Early Career Professionals
**Profile:** 1-5 years in higher education
**Pain Points:** Workplace navigation, institutional culture, career trajectory
**Messaging:** Foundational skills, career development

### Segment 2: Middle Managers
**Profile:** 5-15 years experience, managing teams
**Pain Points:** Managing up/down, promotion positioning, competing priorities
**Messaging:** Leadership development, strategic positioning

### Segment 3: Senior Leaders
**Profile:** Directors, VPs, Presidents, C-suite
**Pain Points:** Complex challenges, team building, culture transformation
**Messaging:** Executive coaching, organizational development

### Common Thread
Higher education complexity requires expert guidance across all career stages.

### Traffic Sources
- LinkedIn (primary)
- Conference presentations
- Webinars
- Published books
- Professional organizations
- Organic search

## Design & Brand Guidelines

### Brand Personality
Highly credentialed expert who is authentic, accessible, supportive, actionable, unexpected, caring, wise, direct, funny.

**Formality:** 6.5/10 - Professional but approachable

### Visual Design

**Approach:** Clean, content-forward design with strategic visual interest

**Typography:**
- Client brand fonts (from brand kit)
- Generous line spacing
- Clear hierarchy (H1, H2, H3, body)
- Minimum 16px body text

**White Space:**
- Generous margins and padding
- Uncluttered layouts
- Clearly defined sections

**Color:**
- Client brand palette (from brand kit)
- Strategic pops for CTAs
- High contrast (WCAG AA minimum)

**Visual Elements:**
- Custom graphics/icons
- Illustrations/abstract shapes for section breaks
- Minimal photography (1-2 client photos, not prominent)
- No stock photos

**Layout:**
- Scannable section-based design
- Clear visual hierarchy
- Consistent spacing
- Grid-based structure

**Avoid:**
- Slider carousels
- Excessive animation
- Auto-playing video
- Busy backgrounds
- Too many competing CTAs

### Brand Kit
Client provides after hire:
- Logo files
- Color palette (hex codes)
- Typography specs
- Brand personality details
- Design elements to avoid

### Photography
- Maximum 1-2 client photos on entire site
- Not focal points
- No stock photography

### Tone
- Small pops of humor in copy
- Direct, unexpected statements in headlines
- Professional credibility maintained

## Accessibility Requirements

### Compliance
**WCAG 2.1 Level AA** - Required

### Checklist

**Keyboard Navigation:**
- All interactive elements accessible via keyboard
- Logical tab order
- Visible focus indicators
- No keyboard traps

**Screen Readers:**
- Semantic HTML
- ARIA labels where needed
- Alt text for images (descriptive)
- Form labels properly associated
- Skip-to-content link

**Color & Contrast:**
- Minimum 4.5:1 for body text
- 3:1 for large text (18pt+ or 14pt+ bold)
- Color not sole information conveyer
- Links distinguishable from text

**Text:**
- Minimum 16px body text
- Resizable up to 200%
- Line height minimum 1.5
- Paragraph spacing 1.5x line height
- No text in images

**Forms:**
- Clear labels
- Error messages associated with fields
- Required fields indicated
- Helpful placeholders (not replacing labels)

**Multimedia:**
- Captions for videos
- Transcripts for audio
- No auto-playing media

**Interactive Elements:**
- Buttons clearly labeled
- Descriptive link text
- Dropdown menus keyboard accessible
- Modals closeable with Escape key

**Testing:**
- WAVE, Axe, Lighthouse (automated)
- Keyboard-only navigation (manual)
- Screen reader (NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver)
